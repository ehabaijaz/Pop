FAQ

Quick answers before you start.

Will my project get accepted?

If your project is high quality, tracked with hackatime, has regular commits, does not use AI and is related to a fandom of your choice then why not?

What can I ship?

Make an app, website or game that relates to a fandom you like! Submit them for lollipops, the currency of POP.

Am I eligible?

If you're 13 to 18, yes, no matter where you live.

Have more questions?

Join the #pop channel in Slack for info, updates and support!

Can I use AI?

No. Using AI while trying to make something related to your fandom is a recipe for disaster.
